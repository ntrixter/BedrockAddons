## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-16

### Added

- Dropped netherite never despawns: the sword, spear, pickaxe, axe, shovel,
  hoe, the four armour pieces, netherite horse and nautilus armour, ancient
  debris, netherite scrap, ingots, blocks of netherite and the netherite
  upgrade template.
- Dropped items enchanted with Mending never despawn either, whatever they
  are made of.
- Settings, under the gear icon beside the pack: toggles for netherite and for
  Mending items (both on by default), and one that keeps any renamed item as
  well (off by default).
